Questions.

Anything else, email us.

Will people know it’s automated?

Yes. The first message says it’s your business’s automated assistant. People don’t mind a quick, helpful reply. They mind waiting until tomorrow.

What if a lead asks something it doesn’t know?

It tells them a team member will get back to them, and emails you the question straight away. It never guesses prices, availability or anything it hasn’t been told.

Can I jump in myself?

Any time. Open the conversation in your portal and reply, or press Take over. The assistant stops replying to that person.

Where do the leads come from?

Your website forms, Facebook and Instagram lead forms, and texts to your number. If you use another form or CRM, we can usually connect it too.

Will my leads see your name anywhere?

No. It’s fully white-labelled. Texts come from your number, emails carry your logo, colours and sign-off, and you can give the assistant its own name. On the Team plan, emails can come from your own domain too.

Do I need to be a marketing client?

No. Reply works on its own. If you’re also a marketing client, every lead we bring in gets answered straight away.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Month to month with 30 days’ notice in writing.
