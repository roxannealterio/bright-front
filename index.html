How we see it

Usually it isn't broken.It just needs a revamp.

Most businesses outgrow their branding without noticing. The work gets better every year and the logo, the website and the marketing stay exactly where they were five years ago.

Starting again is rarely the answer. The brand gets tidied up, the website gets rebuilt around what people came there to do, and someone keeps all of it moving.

Design that only looks good is half the job. We came to this from sales, so what we care about is the thirty seconds after someone lands on your page, and whether they call, book or order.

Brightfront is Roxanne Alterio, and you work with her from the first call to the day it goes live. We go through what you want in detail, down to the parts you can picture but can't describe. Then we build it, you see it early, and you say what changes.

    Before you ask

    Questions.

    Something not covered? Book a free 15-minute call and ask.

    Do I own everything?

    Yes, all of it. Domain, designs, words, photos, ad accounts, all in your name. If you walk away it all goes with you.

    What if I don't like the first draft?

    Tell us what's not working and we'll change it. If the whole direction's wrong, we start again once and don't charge you for it.

    Does my business need a rebrand?

    Often not. If people already recognise your logo, a refresh of the site and how you present it costs less and does more. We'll tell you that on the first call even though it's the smaller job.

    Can't you just do a logo?

    We can, and sometimes that really is all it takes. Just know a new logo won't fix a website that's five years behind it. People notice that sort of thing.

    What if I don't know what I need yet?

    That's what the strategy session is for. Ninety minutes, $350, and you walk away with the plan whether or not you hire us to build it. The first fifteen-minute call is still free, so ask us anything there first.

    Do you offer payment plans?

    Half to start, half the day you go live. Bigger jobs can be split monthly. Ask on the call.

    Are you only in Brisbane?

    We're in Brisbane and happy to meet up. Most of the work happens over email and video calls though, so it doesn't much matter where you are.
