FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t see yours? Email help@autofice.com.au.

Is Tilly a real person?

No. Tilly is an AI admin. She introduces herself as your business’s automated assistant, and hands anything she can’t answer to you.

How does Tilly know when I’m free?

She reads your Google or Outlook calendar to see your real availability, only offers times you’re free, and adds the booking straight in. Block out time in your calendar and she won’t book over it.

Do I need Xero?

No. Tilly answers enquiries, calls and DMs and follows up quotes without it. Connect Xero and she also chases overdue invoices and stops the moment they’re paid.

How does invoice chasing work?

Tilly checks Xero for overdue invoices each weekday and sends a friendly reminder 1, 7 and 14 days after the due date, with Xero’s pay-online link. If the customer replies, she answers them and lets you know.

What if a customer asks about price?

Tilly only shares prices you’ve told her she can. Anything else, she says you’ll confirm and passes it to you with a summary.

Do I need a new phone number?

We set you up with an Australian number for Tilly. Calls can ring your mobile first, and Tilly picks up when you can’t. Prefer your current number? Forward unanswered calls to her.

Does it work with my job software?

Tilly works with Xero, Google Calendar and ServiceM8, and can send leads to most other tools, like Tradify or Fergus, through Zapier or Make.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Plans are month to month with 30 days’ notice, after a one-off setup fee.
