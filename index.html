Questions, answered

Can’t see yours? Email info@brightfrontstudio.com

Do you actually call our leads?

Yes. Our AI answers every enquiry in under a minute, then our sales team calls the ones that need a person. We qualify them, book them into your calendar and follow up no-shows. You see every call and booking in your client portal.

How long before I see something?

Paid media moves in weeks. SEO takes months. Anyone promising a specific number by a specific date is guessing. Within the first month you’ll have a plan, tracking configured and campaigns live.

Do I have to take every channel?

No. Most clients start with one or two. The system is how the pieces are built to fit together, not a minimum order.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. Google Ads, Meta Business, Analytics, your domain and hosting, all in your name from day one. If we stop working together you keep them, with the history intact.

How is ad spend handled?

Your card sits on your own Google and Meta accounts, so they bill you directly. We never take a percentage of spend and never mark it up.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes, 3 months. That’s how long it takes for campaigns and follow-up to show real results. After that it’s month to month with 30 days’ notice in writing, and no exit fees.

What size business is this for?

Established businesses that have outgrown doing their own marketing. Above roughly $50k a month in ad spend, a big agency will serve you better. Below that, you get more attention here.
