Questions about Tilly

Can’t see yours? Email info@brightfrontstudio.com

Is Tilly a real person?

No. Tilly is an AI admin. She introduces herself as your business’s automated assistant, and hands anything she can’t answer to you.

Do I need a new phone number?

We set you up with an Australian business number for Tilly. Put it on your website, van and Google profile, and calls ring your mobile first. Prefer to keep your current number? Set it to forward unanswered calls to your Tilly number and she’ll text them back.

What if a customer asks about price?

Tilly only shares prices you’ve told her she can. Anything else, she says you’ll confirm and passes the lead to you with a summary.

How does invoice chasing work?

Connect your Xero account. Tilly checks for overdue invoices each weekday and sends a friendly reminder 1, 7 and 14 days after the due date, with Xero’s pay-online link. She stops as soon as it’s paid or the customer replies, and you can turn it off any time.

Does it work with my job software?

Tilly works with Google Calendar, ServiceM8 and Xero, and can send leads to most other tools, like Tradify or Fergus, through Zapier or Make.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Tilly plans are month to month with 30 days’ notice, after a one-off setup fee.
